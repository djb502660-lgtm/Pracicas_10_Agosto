### WEEK 1: FROM JUNE 15 TO JUNE 19
* **Essential Contents:** 
  * Greetings and farewells
  * Personal information
  * Verb To Be in affirmative, negative and interrogative forms
  * WH Questions (What, Where, Who, How old)
* **Skills with Performance Criteria:**
  * **EFL 4.2.10.** Sustain a conversational exchange on a familiar, everyday subject when carrying out a collaborative/paired learning activity in which there are specific instructions for a task.
  * **EFL 4.2.16.** Initiate, maintain and end a conversation to satisfy basic needs and/or handle a simple transaction.
* **Evaluation Indicators:**
  * **I.EFL.4.10.1.** Learners can effectively participate in familiar and predictable everyday conversational exchanges in order to complete a task, satisfy a need or handle a simple transaction, using a range of repair strategies. (Example: asking for clarification, etc.) (I.3, J.3, J.4)
* **Active Methodological Strategies for Teaching and Learning:**
  * **Greeting / Class Objective**
  * **Experience:** Dynamic greeting in English. "Find someone who..." game. Introductions exercise: "Hi, I'm... What's your name?" / "Hello. My name is... Nice to meet you."
  * **Reflection:** Students identify personal information (Mr., Mrs., Miss) using visual prompts (1. ___ Lopez, 2. ___ Molina, 3. ___ Vargas). Complete introductions with *he - she - his or her* (e.g., "This is Mr. Lopez. He is our Headmaster. His first name is Alberto.", "This is Mrs. Molina. She is our Secretary. Her office is on the first floor.").
  * **Conceptualization:** Guided explanation of the verb "to be" and WH questions (Grammar Boost: Positive statements, Negative statements, Yes/No questions, Wh-questions). Reference link: [Forming WH Questions with To Be](https://www.businessenglishresources.com/learn-english-for-business/teachers-section/grammar-lessons-2/forming-questions-part-1-wh-questions-with-to-be/)
  * **Application:** Peer interviews. Personal oral presentation. Complete page 8.
* **Evaluation Activities:**
  * **Technique:** Rubric
  * **Instrument:** Analytical Rubric
  * **Criteria:** 
    * Use the Verb To Be correctly.
    * Formulate WH questions appropriately.
    * Pronounce personal information clearly.
    * Actively participates in oral interaction.
* **Resources:** English textbook, Flashcards, QR audio, Whiteboard, Markers.
